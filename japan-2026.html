Late May – Early June 2026

Japan

← Back to all trips

Two weeks built around the KTCC2026 conference in Kyoto, with a multi-day hike on the Kumano Kodo before it and sightseeing around Nara and Osaka after.

The route

  • Kyoto — KTCC2026 conference, temples and the city between sessions
  • Kumano Kodo, Nakahechi route — the multi-day pilgrim trail
  • Nachi — the shrine and waterfall day, worked around the hike's logistics
  • Nara — the deer park and the old temples
  • Osaka — the last stop before flying home

Preparation included phrase guides, packing lists, real-time train navigation across the Kintetsu and JR Yamatoji lines, and forwarding luggage ahead via Takkyubin so the hiking stretch could be done without full packs.
