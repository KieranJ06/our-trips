A week on the island, mostly spent walking the levadas — the old irrigation channels that thread across Madeira and double as some of its best trails.

The route

  • Funchal — home base, old town, the market
  • Levada walks — several routes through the laurel forest and terraced hills
  • The north coast — a different, wilder stretch of the island
