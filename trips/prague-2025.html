19–23 November 2025

Prague

← Back to all trips

Wednesday 19th November

The trip technically began the night before it began — a drive down to Heathrow and a night in an airport hotel to make sure our early flight didn't start with a scramble. We settled into the room, ordered Domino's, and let Schitt's Creek run on Kieran's laptop until it was time to force ourselves to sleep before an absurdly early alarm.

Thursday 20th November

The 06:45 flight meant a brutal wake-up, but it paid off — security was about as fast and painless as it's ever been for us. And as tradition demands, a Pret cookie was secured before boarding, officially marking the start of the holiday.

Flight, Uber, bags dropped at the hotel — and then a small piece of drama we weren't expecting: our Uber got pulled over and checked by security forces near the hotel, since we were staying right by the US Embassy. A new one for us.

With bags stashed, we went straight into exploring our side of the river and walked the length of Charles Bridge. Prague Castle turned out to be a running joke for the rest of the day — it isn't one building but a sprawling complex, and Laura spent a good while searching for "the castle" before realising we'd been standing inside it the whole time. To warm back up, we found hot chocolate so thick it was basically drinking brownie batter — genuinely the best either of us had ever had.

For a drink, Max's recommendation came through: U Glaubicu, where six beers cost us a tenner — the price of two back home. After that, back to the hotel to check in properly (the room turned out to be enormous), a quick change, and out again to the other side of the bridge, timed just right to catch the astronomical clock chime.

Dinner was burgers at an American pop-culture-and-music-themed spot — Laura's "smash" burger arrived looking nothing like a smashed burger and far too big for one person, and the buffalo chicken on the side pushed it well over the edge. We walked it off on the way back to the hotel and closed the day the same way we'd started it: Schitt's Creek in bed, then a very welcome early night.

Friday 21st November

Breakfast at the hotel came with a lesson learned — Kieran's choice of sausages turned out to be three strange, tiny things, while Laura's "egg and everything else" was the clear winner of the table.

Out exploring on a properly cold day, we spotted a nutria along the river — somewhere between a beaver and a rat, and not an animal either of us had seen before. A snack stop at Sausage Go kept us going, before a detour into a wine shop for Laura's inevitable tasty-wine fix: some Czech white at a French wine bar, paired with cheese. The drinks theme carried on into a craft beer bar, where a Czech cherry sour from Wild Creatures was a genuine highlight.

With the cold setting in properly, U Glaubicu called us back — it felt like exactly the right vibe for the weather. Laura's navigation had other ideas, walking us down several wrong streets before we finally arrived, but once there we settled into traditional Czech food (pork knuckle with dumplings, chicken schnitzel), several very cheap Pilsner Urquells, and a few games. Kieran's performance on the games front was, to put it kindly, dismal — four games, four losses. We stumbled the short walk back to the hotel and called it a night.

Saturday 22nd November

Another early start (no tiny sausages for Kieran this time) and a train out to Kutná Hora to see the Sedlec Ossuary — the Bone Church, and its collection of over 40,000 human bones.

The day was cold but dry and sunny, ideal for wandering the town centre on the way to St Barbara's Church, which more than lived up to expectations. Lunch took some Googling to track down, but was good and cheap once we found it — Laura's fried cheese in particular — even if the service was memorably odd.

The walk back to the train ran longer than planned after Laura led us to the wrong station, though we still made our train with time to spare — the extra fifteen minutes just weren't especially welcome in the cold. We then sat in the wrong carriage entirely, packed in among a full train, until a distinctly unimpressed conductor moved us along. All told: a fine day out, worth doing, but not one we'd have grieved skipping if something better had come up.

Back in Prague, we returned to the craft beer bar from the day before, where the bartender recognised us on sight. What was meant to be a quick sausage snack turned into the whole evening — games, more drinks than planned, and a genuinely great time. When we finally asked for the bill, the bartender brought over shots and told us, with real admiration, how surprised he was at how much we'd had to drink given how fine we still seemed. More Sausage Go and a portion of frites completed the walk home.

Sunday 23rd November

Minus eleven degrees when we woke up. Minus eleven. One last early hotel breakfast and off to the airport, trip complete.

Highlights

  • The evening at U Glaubicu — proper food, proper drink, a great atmosphere despite the tourist crowd, and the kind of chilled-out night where you completely lose track of time
  • The craft beer bar, both visits — great drinks and a bartender who remembered us and clearly enjoyed watching us drink him out of shots
  • Sausage Go, repeatedly, without shame
