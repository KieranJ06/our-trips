Slovenia

← Back to all trips

A week and a half through the Julian Alps and the Karst, starting in Ljubljana and working out to Lake Bled, the cave systems near the Italian border, and finishing on the coast at Piran.

The route

  • Ljubljana — old town, river walks, a first taste of Slovenian food
  • Lake Bled — the island church, the castle above the lake, the walk around the shore
  • Bohinj — quieter and greener than Bled, good base for the surrounding hills
  • Postojna Cave — the underground railway and the show caverns
  • Škocjan Caves — the river canyon underground, more dramatic than Postojna
  • Predjama Castle — built into the cliff face above its own cave
  • Piran — the coast, narrow streets, the last stop before heading home

Prepared with a short Slovenian phrasebook put together beforehand — enough for directions, menus, and basic pleasantries along the way.
