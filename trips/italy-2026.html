Easter

Bologna & the Via degli Dei

← Back to all trips

Based in Bologna over Easter, with the Via degli Dei — the old trading path over the Apennines toward Florence — as the main event, alongside day trips to Florence and Modena.

The route

  • Bologna — home base, porticoes, and the food the region is known for
  • Via degli Dei — the multi-day hiking route through the hills toward Florence
  • Florence — a day trip in from Bologna
  • Modena — a shorter day trip, more food than sightseeing

Language prep for this one used a Spanish-scaffolded Italian phrasebook, leaning on the overlap between the two rather than starting from nothing.
